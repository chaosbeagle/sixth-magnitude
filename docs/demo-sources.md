# Demo images

The slide screenshots use two public whole-slide images from the [OpenSlide test-data collection](https://openslide.cs.cmu.edu/download/openslide-testdata/). Their individual reuse terms are listed below and in the collection's [per-file metadata](https://openslide.cs.cmu.edu/download/openslide-testdata/index.json).

| Slide | Source | Credit and reuse terms |
|---|---|---|
| Huron slide | [Huron-1.tif](https://openslide.cs.cmu.edu/download/openslide-testdata/Huron/Huron-1.tif), H&E image scanned at 20× | Huron Digital Pathology; [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| Aorta slide | [JP2K-33003-1.svs](https://openslide.cs.cmu.edu/download/openslide-testdata/Aperio/JP2K-33003-1.svs), aorta tissue, Aperio brightfield image | OpenSlide test-data collection; the [source listing](https://openslide.cs.cmu.edu/download/openslide-testdata/Aperio/) permits use and distribution, with or without modification. No individual creator is listed. |

The screenshots use the general inspection workspace to show navigation, region selection, observations and saved overlays. The overlays and observations were added for the demonstration. They are not supplied diagnoses or reference annotations, and the source metadata does not establish a lung cancer diagnosis. The lung-pattern territory example uses a separate synthetic diagram.

The original slide files are downloaded separately. The repository includes the screenshots and an SVG illustration. The demo command generates a practice image locally; the illustration and practice image are synthetic. Reuse of the slide imagery follows the terms in the table, separately from the project code license.
